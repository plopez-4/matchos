## What participants do

1. Give football-viewing frequency and the device they are using to view the form.
2. Look at Screenshot 1 (main screen), explain the apparent purpose, and name where they would click to catch up.
3. Read Screenshot 2 (actual recap), identify the leading team and describe the goal's build-up. Interpret the schematic pitch markers.
4. Look at Screenshot 3 (actual notification), explain the incident, identify its match and describe the expected button destination.
5. Identify which visible recap action they would select to examine the goal. Rate recap clarity/confidence, give a hypothetical tune-in decision and reasoning, and describe confusion or missing information.

Participants should answer in order and may zoom the images. Do not explain the features or correct their understanding before they finish. Do not ask them to report actions they have not performed. No questions claim they have used the app.

## Owner-only answer key

- Apparent purpose: helping a fan understand missed match action and decide what to follow; accept equivalent explanations.
- Main screen catch-up destination: Catch Me Up / Catch me up.
- Displayed recap: Manchester City lead Manchester United 1–0 at 45:30. It describes Rico Lewis recovering at 40:00, a five-pass team sequence involving Phil Foden, Ruben Dias, Josko Gvardiol and Mateo Kovacic, then Jeremy Doku's shot at 40:10 and goal at 40:11. The five passes are a team sequence, not five passes by Lewis. This is comprehension of the shown paragraph, not an independent audit of AI factual accuracy.
- Pitch markers: recorded action locations connected in a schematic replay, not continuous tracking or prediction.
- Alert: Fulham's Joachim Andersen sent off at 60:00 in Brighton–Fulham, 0–0. Expected destination: that fixture's recorded red-card moment, not a forecast or live broadcast video.
- Recap follow-up: Watch Jeremy Doku's goal / Inspect supporting events. Accept either if their explanation matches the visible action.
- Tune-in answer: Yes, No and Not sure are all valid. Assess the explanation, not agreement with the product idea.
