# Screenshot questionnaire — findings reviewed 25 October 2026

Scope: ten Google Forms submissions reviewed on 25 October. This is a saved review of that set, not a claim about later responses or ten independently verified unique fans. Respondents interpreted screenshots; they did not perform app tasks. No names, raw response exports, identifiers or verbatim participant quotes are published here.

| Question or measure                                | Result in reviewed submissions            |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Device reported                                    | 8 phone, 1 tablet, 1 desktop              |
| Identified Catch Me Up as the catch-up destination | 6/10                                      |
| Selected the named goal action                     | 8/10                                      |
| Interpreted the pitch as a recorded schematic      | 6/10; 3 continuous tracking, 1 prediction |
| Interpreted the alert as a recorded sending-off    | 8/10; 1 winner prediction, 1 live video   |
| Recap clarity rated 4 or 5 out of 5                | 8/10; mean 4.1                            |
| Confidence rated 4 or 5 out of 5                   | 8/10; mean 4.1                            |
| Hypothetical willingness to tune in                | 6 yes, 4 unsure, 0 no                     |

At least four responses explicitly expected actual video or a clip/recording of the incident. Ambiguous mentions of replay were not all classified as video expectations. One response confused the different fixture in the notification screenshot. These findings do not establish whether any respondent would actually watch a match.

## Changes informed by the feedback

- Made Catch Me Up a more prominent primary action.
- Named goal actions as replaying the goal **build-up**, with schematic-replay wording.
- Labeled notifications from another fixture more explicitly.
- Replaced sending-off pitch animations with recorded explanations and inspectable event evidence.
- Kept the main fan page separate from the development dashboard; compacted both for mobile.

The revised interface has not been retested with the same questionnaire group. The owner separately confirmed the current computer and phone layouts. Do not convert these findings into a usability success rate, real-world engagement uplift or independent audit of AI factual accuracy. The [hands-on protocol](../fan-evaluation.md) is a separate proposed study.
