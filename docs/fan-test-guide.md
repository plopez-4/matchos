# MatchOS formative fan test

Use with 6–8 consenting football fans. This is a small usability pilot, not proof of engagement uplift. Use fictional match replays; the app does not provide live video.

## Session (about 20 minutes)

1. Ask which kinds of games they usually follow. Explain that they can stay with their current match, choose another, or ignore recommendations.
2. Show a compact score/timeline view and the new trajectory card with equivalent observed facts. Alternate which version comes first between participants; use matched scenarios so remembering a goal does not determine the next choice.
3. Include a repeated-equalizer match, a quiet draw and a one-sided scoring run. Pause at the same observed clock when comparing views. Do not invent a delayed-feed warning; missing-source detection has not been implemented.
4. Ask: "What happened? Who led? Did the other team ever lead? Why would you follow or decline this game? What information did you infer that the page did not state?"
5. Let them follow the match and inspect the timeline. Ask whether the explanation matched what they found, whether the evidence drawer helped, and whether the notification was distracting.
